# Volumes 4–9 Main Story Summary: Creation Reference Draft

**Languages:** [繁體中文](../zh-TW/main-story-user-outline.md) · [English](main-story-user-outline.md) · [日本語](../ja/main-story-user-outline.md)

The following preserves the user-provided story summary as-is, and may contain reader interpretations, speculation, and details not verified chapter by chapter. It is a creation reference for [主線續寫](mainline-continuation.md), **not** verified verbatim original text, nor a list of the implementations of every event currently in the game. During development, you should separately annotate what is verifiable from the original work, what is fan re-writing, and what still needs to be verified; do not stuff this summary directly into in-game characters' or official accounts' self-introductions.

---

Okay. Let me explain first: below will be **major spoilers for volumes 4–9**. I'll organize each volume as "event starting point → the real problem → how Warm Water (Junnosuke) gets dragged in → climax → wrap-up → its meaning for the later romance lines", rather than retelling chapter by chapter from the novel.

Also, the titles you listed can be slightly refined to:

**4: Tsukinoki's doujinshi confiscation incident + Shikiya/Koto relationship repair**
**5: Yoshiki's Valentine's chocolate + Momozono Junior High infiltration**
**6: Lemon's literature-club withdrawal crisis + 100-meter final**
**7: Shiraishiro Riko's wedding sabotage/reclamation operation**
**8: Aisaki's student-council president election + confession**
**9: Five-person Hida trip + Hori/Sakurai/Koharu love triangle**

---

# 1. Volume 4: The Confiscated Book and Shikiya's Proposal

The surface crisis in Volume 4 is actually quite absurd:

**A real-person-themed BL doujinshi created by the senior Tsukinoki Koto is confiscated by the student council's Matori Aisaki.**

The real trouble is that this is not just "getting back a rule-violating book".

Because Tsukinoki is a former literature-club president and the content involves people on campus, Aisaki thinks the situation is serious enough that further action might be needed; if it escalates to a faculty meeting, the literature club itself could be held accountable.

The official volume synopsis itself begins the entire volume with "Tsukinoki's real-person-themed BL book confiscated by the student council, Aisaki preparing further action". ([Ruten Marketplace][1])

## Shikiya Suddenly Proposes a Really Strange Plan

At this point the senior Shikiya Yumeko appears.

She says to Junnosuke:

**"How about we conquer Aisaki?"**

Of course, this is not telling Junnosuke to actually fall in love with Aisaki.

Shikiya's meaning is closer to: use Aisaki's personality, interests, and psychological weaknesses to make her change her stance.

So Junnosuke, who had only just become the literature-club president, unexpectedly takes on his first truly "president's mission".

And Yanami immediately goes on alert.

She is very clear on one thing:

> Every time Junnosuke says "I'm just going to fix things", he somehow ends up getting closer to another girl.

So Yanami begins a pattern that becomes important later:

**"You can help, but you're not allowed to sneak ahead."**

---

## As It Turns Out, Aisaki Has a Problem of Her Own

After Junnosuke interacts with Aisaki, he realizes:

This girl appears extremely serious, rigid, and rule-abiding on the surface, but is actually very easily led astray by odd things.

As a result, after she comes into contact with Tsukinoki's work...

In a sense, she begins to understand BL.

So the situation shifts from:

**"Get the rule-violating book back"**

gradually to:

**"How to handle the relationship between Aisaki, Tsukinoki, and Shikiya."**

Finally Aisaki gives a condition:

Before the closing ceremony,

**make Shikiya Yumeko and Tsukinoki Koto settle their issue.** ([Bahamut Forum][2])

This reveals the true main line of Volume 4.

---

# What Shikiya Really Wants to Resolve Is Tsukinoki

Shikiya and Tsukinoki once had a very deep relationship.

But between the two of them a rift formed over certain past events.

On the surface Shikiya always seems the type who says:

"Hmm... whatever..."

"I dunno..."

A ghostly person with no emotional ups and downs.

Actually, she does care.

On the contrary, she has a deep obsession with Tsukinoki.

Shikiya just doesn't know:

**how to return to their old relationship.**

So she pushes Junnosuke into the event, essentially using him as an intermediary.

This is also a very important ability of Junnosuke's character:

He is not a master of romance.

But he is very good at being:

**"a bridge between two people who cannot talk directly."**

---

# The Truly Important Point of Volume 4

Surface:

> Rescue the BL book.

Actual:

> The repair of the Shikiya–Tsukinoki relationship.

One level deeper:

> Junnosuke formally begins to become "someone who can handle other people's relationship problems".

And this volume does something very important for later:

**Establishes Junnosuke × Aisaki.**

Originally Aisaki and Junnosuke were not even in the same world.

After this incident, she starts to form a very strong impression of Junnosuke.

This line won't explode until Volume 8.

---

# 2. Volume 5: Yoshiki's Valentine's Chocolate Mystery

At first glance Volume 5 doesn't look like a romance main line.

Actually, it turns out to be the series' most classic:

**The sister-con-loving brother's breakdown chapter.**

Valentine's Day is coming.

Yoshiki is at home test-making chocolate.

Everything was normal at first.

Until Yoshiki reveals:

**this year there is "someone I want to give chocolate to".**

Junnosuke:

> ………

And that someone looks like...

**not her brother.**

The official synopsis makes this the core of the entire volume:

"Yoshiki's handmade chocolate recipient is not her brother?" ([Light Novel Library][3])

---

# Junnosuke Starts Frantically Deceiving Himself

Junnosuke keeps insisting:

> I'm not a sister-con.

But inside he starts:

* Is it a boy?
* Isn't second year of junior high too early?
* Isn't a honmei chocolate inappropriate?
* Has Yoshiki been fooled by some strange man?

Then he overhears that Yoshiki seems to have a date with some male classmate.

Junnosuke completely freezes.

He even asks Yanami and Komari for help.

The two react very normally:

> "Your sister probably has a crush on someone."

Junnosuke:

**You guys have no idea how serious this is.**

The funny thing about this volume is right here:

Usually it's Yanami, Komari, and Lemon getting messed up by romance problems.

This time it's Junnosuke.

---

# Sakigake Proposes the Craziest Plan

Lemon proposes:

**go directly to Yoshiki's junior high and investigate.**

It happens to coincide with Momozono Junior High's school visitation event.

So Junnosuke really finds a way to infiltrate Yoshiki's school.

The whole volume becomes:

> A big brother detective investigating his sister's Valentine's target.

And his behavior has become so absurd that everyone else in the literature club can tell:

You are obviously super hung up on Yoshiki.

---

# But Things Are Not What Junnosuke Thinks

The important point of Volume 5 is:

Yoshiki did not suddenly find "a man to replace her brother".

The entire chocolate event includes Yoshiki's own feelings for her brother, her growth, and her relationships with the people around her.

The author even directly calls this volume in the afterword:

**a volume that reveals the secret of Yoshiki's feelings for her brother.** ([Light Novel Encyclopedia][4])

And the most interesting part of the story is not "who exactly Yoshiki gives it to".

Rather, it's Junnosuke for the first time facing the fact:

**Yoshiki cannot be the little sister who just clings to me forever.**

She has her own friends.

She has her own life.

And she will slowly grow up.

---

# Meanwhile Aisaki Starts Quietly Gaining Points

Remember Volume 4?

The relationship between Aisaki and Junnosuke didn't end.

In Volume 5 she comes to find Junnosuke again.

And Valentine's Day itself is one of the biggest events of a romantic comedy.

Aisaki is actually trying to get closer to Junnosuke.

It's just...

Junnosuke's mind is completely filled with:

**"Which man is Yoshiki giving her chocolate to?"**

So Aisaki's advances are basically completely ignored by big-brother mode.

([Bahamut Forum][5])

This also makes looking back at Volume 8 especially interesting:

Aisaki didn't suddenly start liking Junnosuke.

The author was slowly laying the groundwork in Volumes 4 and 5.

---

# 3. Volume 6: Lemon's Withdrawal and the 100-Meter Battle

Volume 6 is the second time Sakigake Lemon truly becomes the central figure.

It opens with a huge event.

Lemon suddenly says to Junnosuke:

**"Warm Water, go on a date with me."**

The two go to the Take Island Aquarium.

The official synopsis also directly uses the aquarium first date as the opening of the volume. ([Douban Reading][6])

On the surface it looks like:

> Has Lemon finally started to go after Junnosuke?

But actually this is not simply romance.

---

# Lemon Falls into a Life-Direction Crisis

Lemon has always been a mainstay of the track team.

And the literature club is another very important place of belonging for her.

The problem is:

**Both are important, and both need her.**

She starts to think:

Is it too greedy of me to keep staying in the literature club?

Should I put all my time into track?

Plus the pressure of further schooling, competition results, and the expectations of those around her, she gradually leans toward:

**withdrawing from the literature club.**

---

# Why Can't Junnosuke Accept This?

This is very important here.

Junnosuke isn't doing it because:

> "Lemon is part of my harem, so she can't leave."

Rather, for him:

**the literature club has become their home.**

After the Volume 3 Komari incident, Junnosuke truly acknowledged:

He is the literature-club president.

So now that a member is leaving because of pain,

he can't just say:

> All right then, goodbye.

Even though he knows he doesn't understand competitive track at all.

And has no right to say to Lemon, who has worked hard for years:

> You can just do both.

He even knows his own words are irresponsible. ([Bahamut Forum][7])

But he still doesn't want to let Lemon disappear like that.

---

# So Lemon Proposes a Method Most Suited to Her

**A race.**

No big life talk needed.

No analysis of feelings.

Sakigake Lemon is an athlete.

So decide it with the thing she knows best.

**A 100-meter sprint.**

The conditions are roughly:

### If Junnosuke Wins

Lemon doesn't withdraw from the literature club.

She'll seriously balance track and the literature club.

### If Lemon Wins

Junnosuke withdraws too.

Both become "go-home club".

([Bahamut Forum][7])

This condition is actually very heavy.

Because Lemon knows:

**Junnosuke can't easily give up the literature club.**

So this is not just playing a game.

It's two people wagering their own sense of belonging.

---

# The Biggest Problem: How Could Junnosuke Beat Lemon?

An ordinary otaku guy.

Against the school's top track girl.

Under normal circumstances there is no chance at all.

So the whole literature club starts helping Junnosuke.

It becomes a very "Makeine: Too Many Losing Heroines" (Negative Heroine Girl Group):

**hot-blooded sports manga chapter.**

But what really matters in this race isn't the result itself.

It's Junnosuke telling Lemon through action:

> "I really want you to stay."

Before, Junnosuke, when facing other people's life problems, easily stood on the sidelines.

Now he steps onto the field himself.

---

# The Most Important Meaning of Volume 6 for Lemon

The Lemon of Volume 1:

**Lost because Mitsuki chose Chihaya.**

The Lemon of Volume 6 is no longer dealing with that heartbreak.

What she truly faces is:

> "Without Mitsuki, what kind of person do I want to become myself?"

So Volume 6 is actually Lemon's second growth chapter.

The first was getting past her heartbreak.

The second is building her own life.

---

# 4. Volume 7: Riko's Wedding Operation

Volume 7 begins the new school year.

The literature club faces a very real problem:

**Not enough members, possibly disbanded.**

Right then a savior appears:

A first-year student——

# Shiraishiro Riko.

Extremely cute on the outside.

Polite.

Well-behaved.

And she calls Junnosuke:

**"President."**

Junnosuke even thinks:

Finally a normal kouhai has arrived.

Answer:

**Completely wrong.**

---

# Shiraishiro Is Actually a Super Problem Child on School Suspension

She isn't just trying to join the literature club.

She approaches Junnosuke and the others for her own purpose.

And that purpose is related to a wedding.

The official introduction directly calls her:

**a problem child suspended from school for causing trouble**, and calls the whole volume's main line "wedding venue infiltration mission". ([Newton Encyclopedia][8])

---

# Riko's Relationship Problem

Riko falls for someone very important to her.

The problem is:

**that person is getting married.**

So she wants to do something very crazy:

Go to the wedding venue.

On the surface she can package the plan as:

> Want to confirm some things.

But what she truly cannot accept is:

**she hasn't sorted out this feeling yet, and the other person's life is already preparing to move on to the next stage.**

This is why she becomes the new "losing heroine."

---

# The Literature Club Gets Dragged Down by Her

Shiraishiro's most terrifying ability isn't violence.

It's:

**being extremely good at using people.**

She knows:

* Junnosuke has a hard time actually leaving others alone
* Yanami is easily pulled away by atmosphere and food
* Komari is soft inside despite a hard mouth
* Shikiya is super interested in strange things

So it ends up becoming:

**the whole literature club enters the wedding venue together.**

The absurdity escalates throughout.

---

# Volume 7 Is Actually Testing "President Junnosuke"

In the earlier volumes Junnosuke was always saving seniors and classmates.

In Volume 7, for the first time, there's:

**a kouhai he's responsible for.**

Riko keeps calling him "President".

This is very important to Junnosuke.

Because since Volume 3 he's been learning:

> What exactly is a president?

By Volume 7:

Someone actually treats him as "a senpai worth relying on".

---

# What Riko Really Needs Is Not a Successful Wedding Disruption

What she truly needs is:

**someone to accompany her in confirming that this feeling is indeed over.**

This is exactly what *Makeine: Too Many Losing Heroines* repeats again and again.

Losing is not:

> Forget the person right after losing.

It's:

> "I admit that I lost, but my life goes on."

Yanami was like this.

Lemon was like this.

Komari was like this.

Now Riko is too.

---

# Also, Volume 7 Has a Lot of Shikiya Famous Scenes

The wedding environment stimulates Shikiya's "bride" interest.

And there's even very ambiguous Shikiya × Junnosuke interaction.

As a result, Junnosuke uses a super Junnosuke-style logic to stop it:

**bride → must get married → the most important thing about marriage is the marriage registration.**

Shikiya ultimately accepts:

administrative organs have more of a "marriage feel" than romantic gestures.

This deserves to be called one of the most absurd defenses in the series. ([Bahamut Forum][9])

---

# 5. Volume 8: Aisaki's Election Recommender

Volume 8 is one of the volumes with the biggest change in the romance main line so far.

The student council is about to re-elect.

Matori Aisaki is running for:

# student council president.

Then she designates one person as her recommender:

**Junnosuke Kazuhiko.**

The official introduction is:

> Aisaki runs for student council president and insists on naming Junnosuke as her recommender. ([ANIHK][10])

---

# Why Him of All People?

There are plenty of people in the student council who could help Aisaki.

Shikiya even says she's willing.

But Aisaki doesn't want that.

She simply wants:

**Junnosuke to support her.**

At this point it's already obvious:

The recommender is just a surface reason.

What she's really asking is:

> "Will you stand on my side?"

---

# Then a Rival Appears

The other candidate is:

# Sakurai Hiroto.

This is very unfavorable to Aisaki.

Because Sakurai himself:

* Is well-liked
* Is reliably capable
* Has plenty of student-council experience
* Is trusted by many

Even Junnosuke originally thought:

If Sakurai runs,

**he'd probably vote for Sakurai.**

([Bahamut Forum][11])

---

# The Harshest Part: Yanami Stands on the Other Side

Sakurai's recommender:

# Yanami Anko.

So the setup is:

**Aisaki + Junnosuke**

VS

**Sakurai + Yanami**

This design is very harsh.

Because before, Yanami was always by Junnosuke's side.

Now for the first time:

**the two openly stand in opposing camps.**

The official synopsis even makes this a selling point:

> Yanami becomes the other side's recommender, issuing a fair and square challenge to Junnosuke. ([Newton Encyclopedia][8])

---

# Junnosuke Truly Begins to Understand Aisaki

Before, Aisaki was in his eyes:

* A strange vice-president
* Very serious
* Actually dangerously bad at grades
* Easily loses control over BL
* Often misreads his actions

But during the election the two must act together.

Junnosuke gradually sees:

**she really works extremely hard.**

She has many flaws.

Inflexible.

Easily runs wild.

And her social skills are no match for Sakurai's.

But she truly wants to become student council president.

So Junnosuke starts to act not because:

> "I was asked, so I help."

But:

> "I want this person to win."

This difference is very important.

---

# Then Aisaki Directly Breaks the Series' Balance

After the election main line ends,

Aisaki does something the earlier main heroines never truly completed:

# She confesses to Junnosuke.

And it's genuinely serious feelings.

Not a joke.

Not a hint.

Not "no sneaking ahead".

Not "let's go out".

Rather she truly places:

**I like you**

in front of Junnosuke.

---

# Why Didn't Junnosuke Immediately Accept?

This is the biggest key of Volume 8.

His first reaction isn't dislike.

And not even no flutter at all.

On the contrary:

**he really considered it.**

Because objectively:

Aisaki is cute.

Serious.

Likes him.

And he doesn't dislike her either.

"What's wrong with dating?"

Junnosuke even thinks later along the lines of:

> If I'd accepted then, would I have a girlfriend now?

But when he's about to answer——

**the figure of another person rises in his mind.**

The novel deliberately makes the meaning very heavy here.

Aisaki also senses it.

So she doesn't force Junnosuke to say the rejection out loud.

She herself steps back:

**then let's start as friends.**

She hopes to be able to chat and go out more naturally in the future, and hopes that one day Junnosuke truly comes to like her. Related Volume 8 post-reading discussion commonly views this passage as Aisaki sensing that someone unavoidable already exists in Junnosuke's heart. ([Bahamut Forum][12])

---

# Who Is That "Person Who Rose"?

The author did not simply write here:

> "The answer is ○○."

But the narration very clearly pulls the reader's attention to:

# Yanami.

Especially, the whole volume deliberately arranges:

Junnosuke helps Aisaki.

Yanami helps Sakurai.

The two stand on opposite sides.

This instead forces Junnosuke, for the first time, to think:

**what exactly does Yanami mean to me?**

So the biggest meaning of Volume 8 is not:

> Aisaki joins the harem.

Rather:

> **After someone formally confesses to Junnosuke, for the first time Junnosuke can no longer pretend he has no relationship problem at all.**

---

# 6. Volume 9: The Five-Person Hida Trip

Only after Volume 9 was published did the relationships among the previous student-council people suddenly become very complicated.

The five-person trip group is:

* Junnosuke Kazuhiko
* Yanami Anko
* Hori Hibari
* Sakurai Hiroto
* Koharu

The destination:

# the hot-spring area of Hida.

The basic setup of the official public synopsis is:

**Koharu wants to get closer to her boyfriend Sakurai, so she drags Hori, Junnosuke, and Yanami into a five-person trip.** ([Bahamut Forum][13])

---

# Surface Mission: Create a Two-Person World for Koharu and Sakurai

At first it looks very simple.

Koharu and Sakurai are a couple.

But their relationship seems to progress more slowly than imagined.

So everyone arranges a trip.

One of the plans even is:

**everyone else returns first, finally leaving Koharu and Sakurai alone together.**

That is, a big wingman trip.

But after arriving in Hida,

Junnosuke gradually realizes:

**the real problem isn't just the Koharu and Sakurai pair.**

---

# The Core of the Problem Is Actually Three People

## Koharu

Sakurai's girlfriend.

She wants Sakurai to truly pay attention to her.

And she is very concerned about the relationship between Hori and Sakurai.

## Sakurai

Looks like a very stable, reliable, and understanding person.

He was even a student-council president candidate in Volume 8.

But Volume 9 begins to reveal:

**he is actually not that mature in his own relationship problems.**

## Hori

Former student council president.

Dominant.

Extremely capable.

Always gives the impression of "handling everything well".

But in this trip:

**she is clearly not right.**

The Volume 9 public synopsis deliberately hints at Hori's abnormal state from the start. ([Bahamut Forum][13])

---

# The Three People's Past Is More Complicated Than Junnosuke Thought

Before, Junnosuke/the reader probably understood the situation as:

> Koharu = Sakurai's girlfriend
> Hori = Sakurai's very important senpai/friend

Volume 9 turns it into:

**these three people's emotional relationships have never really been sorted out cleanly.**

Koharu's jealousy is not entirely unfounded.

Hori's abnormality is not simply a senior's concern for a junior.

And Sakurai's biggest problem is:

**he never clearly confirmed his own feelings, yet maintains all kinds of relationships.**

The result makes all three suffer.

Volume 9 post-reading discussion even describes it as some kind of "three-way loss" situation. ([Bahamut Forum][14])

---

# Why Is Volume 9 Actually the "Junnosuke Chapter"?

The surface protagonists are:

Hori / Sakurai / Koharu.

But the author is actually doing a very important mirroring.

## Sakurai

Didn't confirm his feelings in time.

Didn't answer others' feelings appropriately.

The result leaves the relationships dragging on.

## Junnosuke

Was just confessed to by Aisaki in Volume 8.

And then?

**he also didn't truly answer.**

Aisaki just said:

> Then let's be friends for now.

So Junnosuke is actually in a position very similar to Sakurai's.

---

# Sakurai Becomes Junnosuke's Cautionary Tale

This is the biggest use of Volume 9.

Junnosuke sees with his own eyes:

> If you think "this is fine as it is now",
> and keep not confirming your own feelings, not answering others' feelings...

Things don't necessarily get better on their own.

They might even hurt everyone.

So after the Hida incident,

Junnosuke begins to realize:

# "The Aisaki matter cannot stay as it is forever."

This is also one of the most important main-line advances of Volume 9 so far. ([Bahamut Forum][14])

---

# So Volumes 4–9 Are Actually One Continuous Growth Line

If you connect the six volumes you listed, it's actually very beautiful:

| Volume | Surface Event | True Core | Junnosuke's Change |
| ----- | ------- | ---------- | ------------- |
| **4** | BL book confiscated | Shikiya and Tsukinoki | Begins proactively solving others' relationships |
| **5** | Yoshiki's chocolate | Sister starts having her own world | Understands that even close relationships change |
| **6** | Lemon's withdrawal | Lemon's life direction | Steps into the arena himself to keep a friend |
| **7** | Riko's wedding operation | Accepting an unattainable love | Truly becomes a president who can care for kouhai |
| **8** | Student council election | Aisaki likes Junnosuke | First time forced to confront his own romance |
| **9** | Five-person Hida trip | Hori / Sakurai / Koharu | Discovers "not answering is also a choice" |

So what Amemori Takibi (the author) is really playing is not:

**"each volume swaps a girl to go after Junnosuke."**

Rather:

**In the first half he keeps having Junnosuke handle others' romance; by the time he's finished, he discovers Junnosuke has already fallen into romance himself.**

In Volume 4 he can still be a relaxed bystander.

From Volume 6 he starts stepping onto the field for others.

In Volume 7 he has already become someone's "president".

By Volume 8, Aisaki throws the question right back at him:

> **"Then who do you like?"**

And Volume 9 uses Sakurai to tell him:

> **You can't keep not answering forever.**

This is why I regard **Volumes 8→9** as the watershed where *Makeine: Too Many Losing Heroines* moves from a "losing-heroine-girl-group romantic comedy" into **Junnosuke's own romance main line**. ([Bahamut Forum][12])
