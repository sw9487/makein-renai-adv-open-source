# Neutral Action Rendering Skill

> Sanitized for the public/open-source build. Graphic violence, injury, torture, and
> combat gore is intentionally excluded; this skill only guides safe, energetic action
> illustration.

## Purpose

A neutral reference for staging **energetic, conflict-driven, or high-stakes** action
scenes — using motion, pose, and framing for impact without depicting real harm.

## Guiding Principles

- Never depict injury, torture, blood, or harm to a subject.
- Convey action with **motion lines, dynamic pose, and camera angle**, not damage.
- Keep conflicts resolved through effort and resolve, not gore.
- When a request would call for graphic violence, instead produce a **clean, dynamic** action
  composition (e.g., a dodge, a clash of postures, a burst of movement).

## Neutral Action Notes

- **Pose**: lunging, bracing, crouching to spring mid-stride, guard stance, turning.
- **Motion**: motion blur, speed lines, trailing afterimage, tilted frame, low angle.
- **Context**: arena, rooftop, road, workshop — busy but safe environments.
- **Energy**: high contrast, saturated accents, dust and debris without harm.

## Example (neutral)

- **Scene**: 一名角色在屋頂上跳躍閃避
  - prompt: 1girl, [character trigger], mid-air, jump, dynamic pose, motion blur, rooftop,
    low angle, energetic, anime coloring
  - negative_prompt: speech bubble, comic, blood, injury, gore, weapon, torture
