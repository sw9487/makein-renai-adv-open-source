# Neutral Character Rendering Skill

> Sanitized for the public/open-source build. Explicit, sexual, or adult content is
> intentionally excluded; this skill only guides safe, tasteful character illustration.

## Purpose

A neutral reference for generating appropriate character illustrations with deliberate
control over **posing, facial expression, and framing** — so rendered scenes read clearly
and stay safe for general audiences.

## Guiding Principles

- Always keep the output tasteful and non-graphic. Do not add explicit or suggestive detail.
- Prefer **implied emotion** over any explicit depiction.
- Keep poses natural and unforced; never write degrading or objectifying instructions.
- Keep subjects appropriately clothed and posed; avoid nudity, revealing states, or
  situations that demean the subject.
- When a request would call for adult content, instead produce a neutral, comfortable
  composition (e.g., a warm conversation, a respectful distance, an everyday gesture).

## Neutral Composition Notes

- **Posing**: standing, sitting, walking, leaning, waving, holding a prop — any natural pose.
  Avoid anything that demeans or sexualizes the subject.
- **Expression**: happy, shy, focused, surprised, thoughtful, smiling, laughing, calm,
  determined — use these freely.
- **Framing**: portrait, full body, profile, three-quarter view, over-the-shoulder, close-up
  of the face to emphasize emotion.
- **Mood**: warm, cozy, energetic, solemn, peaceful — choose lighting and palette to match.
