# Neutral Atmospheric Horror Skill

> Sanitized for the public/open-source build. Body horror, dismemberment, gore, and
> graphic grotesque content is intentionally excluded; this skill only guides safe,
> atmospheric horror and mystery illustration.

## Purpose

A neutral reference for rendering **eerie, unsettling, or mysterious** scenes through
atmosphere and mood — shadow, composition, and stillness — rather than graphic horror.

## Guiding Principles

- Never depict injury, mutilation, dismemberment, corpses, or body horror.
- Build unease with **darkness, haze, isolation, and architecture**, not gore.
- Keep subjects intact and recognizable; prefer psychological dread over graphic shock.
- When a request would call for grotesque content, instead produce a **moody, mysterious**
  composition (e.g., a foggy hall, a lone figure at the far end of a corridor).

## Neutral Atmosphere Notes

- **Lighting**: single guttering lamp, moonlight shafts, long shadows, deep contrast.
- **Environment**: fog, ruins, winding stair, masked windows, antique furniture, dust motes.
- **Composition**: vast negative space, distant tiny subject, silhouettes, slight fisheye.
- **Palette**: desaturated cold tones, muted greens, faded sepia, near-black.
