# Neutral Dramatic Rendering Skill

> Sanitized for the public/open-source build. Graphic injury, blood, gore, and horror
> content is intentionally excluded; this skill only guides safe, dramatic illustration.

## Purpose

A neutral reference for staging **tense, dramatic, or action-oriented** scenes clearly and
safely — using composition, lighting, and expression to build tension instead of graphic
injury or blood.

## Guiding Principles

- Never depict gore, wounds, blood, dismemberment, or injury.
- Convey danger or tension through **lighting, angle, and body language**, not harm.
- Keep subjects intact and unharmed; favor implied drama over explicit detail.
- When a request would call for graphic content, instead produce a **stylized, non-graphic**
  dramatic composition (e.g., a determined look, a storm backdrop, a tense standoff).

## Neutral Drama Notes

- **Tension**: dramatic shadows, low angle, contrasting color, motion blur, decisive posture.
- **Expression**: determined, alarmed, focused, exhausted, resolute — used to imply stakes.
- **Environment**: storm clouds, rubble, machinery, empty corridor, harsh single light source.
- **Staging**: wide shot for scale, close-up for emotion, diagonal composition for energy.
